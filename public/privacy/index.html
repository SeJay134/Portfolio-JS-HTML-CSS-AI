Who runs this site

This personal portfolio and its browser-based tools are operated by Sergei Patrushev. For questions about this policy or your personal information, email patrushev.s.job@gmail.com.

Information you choose to provide

When Google sign-in is enabled and you choose to sign in, the site uses Google and Supabase Authentication to verify your account. Supabase can store your account identifier, email address, display name, profile image URL, account creation time, and an account access status. We use this information to recognize your account, maintain your sign-in session, and control access to supported tools.

We request basic Google identity information for sign-in, not permission to read your Gmail, Google Drive, contacts, or files. Your Google password is not provided to this site.

If you choose to contact me, the contact form prepares an email draft in your browser. It does not submit the draft to this website. If you send the email through your mail application, I receive the information you include and can use it to reply.

Photo Compressor and File Converter

Selected images and documents are processed in your browser. The active on-device image safety check screens some adult or sexual imagery in supported image inputs only; experimental violence and gore models are not enabled in the public tools. Embedded images in documents and later frames of animated files are not comprehensively screened. Image safety decisions and your content-rules checkbox acknowledgment are handled in the browser and are not stored in a portfolio user-content database.

The site is designed not to upload selected files, image pixels, moderation results, or converted outputs to its servers or to third-party moderation services. Downloads are generated locally. Browser devices retrieve ordinary website scripts and model assets from the site's hosting infrastructure; those asset requests, like other website requests, may generate technical access logs but do not contain the selected file contents. Research model weights are prepared and benchmarked separately and are not requested by the public tools.

Browser limitations, crashes, or installed extensions can affect local processing. You remain responsible for keeping originals and for processing only files you have the right to use.

Optional AI assistant

The optional AI assistant is separate from the Photo Compressor and File Converter. If the assistant is enabled and you send a question, it goes to a Vercel-hosted application endpoint. When the owner-operated chat backend is configured, that endpoint forwards the question over an HTTPS ngrok connection to a self-hosted Flask and Ollama service that generates the answer. This is not on-device chat processing: the submitted text is transmitted to the backend, and the connection providers may process request metadata and technical logs. The chat interface holds its conversation history in page memory; the checked-in server handler forwards each question separately rather than storing a portfolio chat history database. Operational logging and retention of any tunnel or hosting records depend on the providers and runtime configuration.

Do not include passwords, financial details, medical information, or other sensitive content in AI questions. If the assistant is unavailable, no question is sent by the disabled chat interface. Read the relevant provider notices and contact the operator for inquiries about chat data.

Browser storage and technical data

The website uses local browser storage for preferences such as your light or dark theme. When you sign in, Supabase stores authentication session information in the browser so your account can remain signed in. You can sign out and clear browser storage in your browser settings.

The hosting platform and authentication providers can process necessary technical information, such as IP address, browser or device information, request times, and security logs, to deliver and protect the service. Google and Supabase may use their own cookies or similar mechanisms as part of authentication.

Why we use information

Information described above is used to operate the portfolio and tools, authenticate users, prevent misuse, troubleshoot issues, protect the service, and respond to contact requests. Google account information is used for account access, not advertising or selling user profiles.

Service providers and links

Relevant providers include Supabase (account authentication and profile database), Google (Google sign-in), and Vercel (site hosting). When the optional owner-hosted AI assistant is enabled, ngrok also provides the encrypted relay to the owner-operated backend. Their services have their own privacy notices and contractual terms. Links to GitHub, LinkedIn, and other external sites are governed by those sites' policies.

Retention and your choices

There is currently no automatic inactivity-based deletion schedule for Supabase account profiles. Account records generally remain until deletion is requested or otherwise performed, subject to legally required retention or security needs. If you would like to request access to, correction of, or deletion of account information, contact patrushev.s.job@gmail.com. The operator may need to verify that you control the account before acting and will handle requests under applicable law; the request does not automatically delete your Google account. Hosting, authentication and optional chat-relay providers may retain limited technical records on their own schedules. Contact the operator to ask about the currently applicable provider retention settings.

If you believe a child under 13 has provided account information, please contact the operator using the same address. You can also choose not to sign in. Parts of the portfolio remain publicly accessible, while protected tools may require an account.

Policy updates

This policy may be updated to reflect changes to the website or its services. The date at the top of this page indicates when this version took effect. Material changes will be reflected in a revised policy on this page.
